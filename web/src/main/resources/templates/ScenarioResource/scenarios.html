Solar output assumes a cloudless sky throughout, so treat the solar and battery figures as an upper bound rather than a forecast. The battery is modelled as a simple controller that charges in the cheapest band and discharges in the dearest — not a perfect-foresight optimiser, because that is not what a real unit does. One consequence: with both solar and a grid-charge window it fills overnight and exports the midday surplus rather than storing it.
