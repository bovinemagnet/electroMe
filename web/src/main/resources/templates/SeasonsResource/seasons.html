Your consumption does not have one shape. A pool running through summer moves thousands of kilowatt hours into the middle of the day, and the plan that wins on that shape can lose on the winter one. This screen prices every plan over {report.split.label()} and over {report.split.otherLabel()} separately, and asks whether being on the best plan in each beats being on the best plan for the year. The year is cut on month boundaries, because a tariff's own blocks and caps reset monthly at their shortest — cutting mid-month would let one restart in both halves.

{#if report.worthSwitching()}

Switching twice a year would have saved {money:dollars(report.savingFromSwitching())} over this window: {report.bestInFirst.planName()} for {report.split.label()} and {report.bestInSecond.planName()} for {report.split.otherLabel()}, {money:dollars(report.switchingTotal())} together, against {money:dollars(report.bestSingle.wholeYear())} for staying on {report.bestSingle.planName()} all year.

That figure is what switching is worth before what it costs you to do it. Two changeovers a year means two sets of paperwork, two final bills, and any sign-up benefit you can only claim once. Weigh it against the number above rather than assuming it is free.
