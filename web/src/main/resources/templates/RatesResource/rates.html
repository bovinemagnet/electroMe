What each plan charges

The published rates, in cents, as they appear on a fact sheet — not what this household would pay. A dash means the plan has no such charge, which is itself worth seeing: a flat tariff has no evening peak to compare. An asterisk means the rate holds only up to a daily or monthly allowance; hover it for the rate beyond. Sorting by a rate puts plans that charge nothing comparable last, because a tariff with no evening peak has not got the cheapest one.
